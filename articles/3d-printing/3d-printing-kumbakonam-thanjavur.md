## How to prepare your 3D printing request

A useful request gives the reviewer enough information to understand both the model and its purpose. Before uploading, gather these details:

1. **Your model file.** The shop lists STL, OBJ, PLY, 3MF, GLB and glTF uploads. Files are converted and checked as part of its stated workflow.
2. **The intended dimensions.** Include the overall size and units, especially if the part needs to fit another component.
3. **The purpose of each part.** Explain whether it is for display, assembly, movement or another use.
4. **The quantity.** State how many copies of each part you need.
5. **Your preferences.** Include material and colour preferences, with alternatives if you have them.
6. **Your deadline.** Give the date you need the finished parts and ask for confirmation of feasibility.

For an assembly, identify which parts connect. A short note such as “this opening needs to fit my existing switch” is more useful when it includes a measurement or drawing. Do not assume a file alone communicates every fit requirement.

## How much does custom 3D printing cost?

NAVION 3D quotes project printing after file review. Catalogue prices for gifts should not be used to estimate the price of a custom model.

To request a useful quotation, send the actual file, size, quantity and intended use together. Ask what the quotation includes and whether any changes are needed before printing. If your budget is fixed, include it in the enquiry so you can discuss the available options.

For catalogue products, check the current product page and checkout for pricing, personalisation choices and delivery details. Contact NAVION 3D to confirm whether collection is available and arrange the location and timing before travelling.

## Ordering from Thanjavur or elsewhere in Tamil Nadu

You can enquire and submit your project online without travelling to Kumbakonam. If you are ordering from Thanjavur or another town in Tamil Nadu, include your delivery PIN code and the date you need the order when you contact NAVION 3D.

Shipping is available across Tamil Nadu. Confirm the delivery charge and estimated arrival date for your order before payment, particularly for custom project parts. Allow for both making the item and shipping it when planning for a college presentation, birthday or other occasion.

If you prefer to collect your order, contact NAVION 3D to confirm availability and arrange the pickup details in advance.

## Choosing a personalised 3D-printed gift

Start with the detail that makes the gift personal. For a name keychain, confirm the spelling and the selected colour combination. For a photo lithophane, choose a clear, well-lit image and consider how the recipient will display it with light behind it.

If you are buying several gifts for an occasion, send the quantities and personalisation details in an organised list. Ask about the completion date before placing a time-sensitive order.
