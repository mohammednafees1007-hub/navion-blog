# Name, Gyro or Queezy: Choosing a Keychain Gift

The easiest way to choose a keychain gift is to decide what you want the recipient to notice first: their name, the moving design or the shape. NAVION 3D's catalogue includes different options for those preferences.

## Choose a name for a personal detail

A [personalised name keychain](https://shop.navionrobotics.com/personalised-name-keychains) puts the recipient's name at the centre of the gift. This works as an individual keepsake or as a way to distinguish similar bags. Confirm the spelling and the available colour choices before ordering.

For a group order, keep one entry per name and do not assume all names will use an identical layout. Ask about any unusually long text before finalising the selection.

## Choose a gyro for its moving design

The [gyro fidget keychain](https://shop.navionrobotics.com/gyro-fidget-keychains) is listed with moving inner rings and a choice of centres. Browse the current product page to select the version and colour you prefer.

Choose it for the design and tactile interaction. A gift listing is not evidence of a therapeutic benefit or a guarantee of improved concentration. If suitability for a particular recipient matters, ask about the product details before buying.

## Choose Queezy for the shape

The Queezy range includes [heart](https://shop.navionrobotics.com/queezy-heart-keychain) and [hexagon](https://shop.navionrobotics.com/queezy-hexagon-keychain) designs. Compare the current options, quantities and colours on their individual pages. Check whether the selected listing is for one item or a pair.

## Make the final checks

Compare the total quantity, selected options and delivery charge before checkout. If you are buying for a specific date, ask whether the order can arrive in time. Any special packaging or message should be confirmed separately.

NAVION 3D is based in Kumbakonam and ships across Tamil Nadu, including orders for customers in Thanjavur.
