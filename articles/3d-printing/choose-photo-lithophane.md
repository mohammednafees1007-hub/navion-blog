# How to Choose a Photo for a Lithophane Gift

A lithophane photo plate reveals its image when lit from behind. Choosing the photo is therefore part of choosing the gift: think about both the picture itself and how the recipient will display it.

## Start with a clear original image

Use a clear, well-lit photo where the important faces or objects are easy to see. Send the original file if you have it, rather than a screenshot of a screenshot. Check that the subject is in focus and that the details you care about are visible.

Choose an image you have permission to use. If it shows someone else, consider whether they would be comfortable having the image turned into a physical gift.

## Check the crop before committing

Decide which part of the picture matters most. A photograph of a large group and a close-up portrait create different composition choices. Ask how the selected image will fit the available plate shape and whether any edge details will be cropped.

If you send several alternatives, number them and explain your preference. Ask which is suitable instead of assuming the first attachment will be selected. Confirm the final image before the order proceeds.

## Think about the display

The plate needs light behind it to show the image. Check the product listing for what is actually supplied, including whether any stand or lighting is included. Do not assume the product is a complete lamp because a photograph shows it illuminated.

For a gift deadline, confirm production and shipping arrangements in advance. Keep the recipient's delivery details separate from the photo-selection discussion so the approved image is easy to identify.
