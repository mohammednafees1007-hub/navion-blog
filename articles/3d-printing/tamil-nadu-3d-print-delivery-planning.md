# Ordering 3D Prints Across Tamil Nadu: Timing and Delivery

When you need a print for a particular date, consider the whole order journey. The time needed to clarify a model, approve the quotation and make the item is separate from the time needed to deliver it.

NAVION 3D is based in Kumbakonam, serves customers in Thanjavur and ships across Tamil Nadu. Share your town and PIN code when checking delivery, especially for a custom project that is not a standard catalogue order.

## Give an arrival date, not just an event date

For a college demonstration, you may need time to assemble the parts after receiving them. For a gift, you may need time to wrap it or hand it to the recipient. Tell the shop the date you need the parcel in hand and explain any important preparation time.

Ask for confirmation of both production timing and the estimated delivery schedule. An estimate should not be treated as a guaranteed arrival date unless a specific commitment has been made for the order.

## Confirm the full delivery details

Use the order process to provide the recipient name, complete address, PIN code and contact number. Check the spelling of the locality and the building or door number. If you intend to change the address after ordering, contact the shop promptly and confirm whether the change can be accommodated.

For project printing, ask whether delivery is included in the quotation or charged separately. Do not apply a catalogue delivery price to a custom job without confirmation.

## Check the parcel against your order

On arrival, compare the quantities and personalisation details with your confirmation. If something is damaged or incorrect, keep the packaging and contact the shop through its published support process. Read the current returns and defects policy for the applicable terms.

[Contact NAVION 3D](https://shop.navionrobotics.com/contact-us) with your PIN code, requirements and deadline before committing to a time-sensitive order.
